# Era I: The First Hundred Years

## Question

Can a headless simulation produce a coherent, causal, reproducible century of
family, settlement, material, and political history?

## Completion evidence

Era I must produce a readable hundred-year chronicle containing births,
marriages, deaths, migrations, famines, household changes, property transfers,
dynastic succession, and at least one understandable political crisis.

The Era closes only when the featured histories can be reproduced from tagged
source, scenario files, seeds, and recorded commands.
