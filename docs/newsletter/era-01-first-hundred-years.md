# The First Hundred Years

This is the planned source for the substantial Rusting post that closes Era I.
It will be written from the completed cycle records and reproducible histories,
not from memory at the end of the Era.

## Opening Chronicle

To be selected from the completed Era.

## The Era's Question

Can a small headless simulation produce a coherent and explainable century of
history?

## What We Built

Current source notes:

- Cycle 1 established explicit time, mortality, stable events, and a century.
- Cycle 2 added four generations of kinship, partnerships, and households.
- Cycle 3 generated the physical world before its historical witnesses.
- Cycle 4 ran six centuries of separate human and orc history until navigation
  enabled first contact, then selected five settlements for detailed history.

The final synthesis should connect these resolutions rather than presenting
them as unrelated features: generated context constrains macro history, and
macro history becomes the inherited context of local households.

## Rust and Bevy

Current source notes:

- pure Rust world passes produce a versioned surface and place graph;
- a Bevy annual schedule advances aggregate populations and institutions;
- a separate Bevy schedule retains detailed people and households;
- stable domain IDs cross every boundary while Bevy entities remain runtime
  details;
- domain-separated ChaCha streams make structural additions noninterfering;
- golden Seed 42 evidence and fixed cohorts test exact and structural behavior.

## What Emerged

Candidate: in Seed 42, the sea route opened and first contact occurred in Year
293. Continental pilots remembered discovery; island rites remembered the
return of divided kin. Four mixed-lineage populations and a Tidebound culture
survived at Year 600.

## What Went Absurdly Wrong

Candidates:

- the first route-opening design assumed every abstract connection was an
  ocean, making orbital transit inexplicably maritime;
- the first schema almost made religion biological;
- all twenty initial cohort worlds reached contact in a narrow sixteen-year
  window, revealing threshold machinery where we wanted contingency.
